# 100-app manual research baseline

**Composio AI Product Ops take-home · 23 November 2026**

This is the source-by-source desk-research baseline for the 100 apps in the assignment. I selected the labels app by app from vendor documentation, help centers, pricing and vendor repositories. The verdicts describe documented feasibility. They are desk-research judgments pending credential or endpoint testing.

**Reading the access columns.** Test access covers sandboxes, trials and local development. Production access covers a live account or self-managed production instance for the example action. Free includes a limited free path and can still require a user's account and consent. Account means the product and credential path are documented but the minimum qualifying plan is unresolved. Approval and paid labels can coexist with workspace permissions.

**Reading the interface columns.** Broad means multiple core product areas. Moderate means several operations in one product area. Narrow means a small, focused interface. MCP labels refer to the named product or the scope described in the row. A developer MCP may provide documentation or development tooling. A client consumes external MCP servers. An unconfirmed server is an evidence gap.

**Evidence limits.** Search-indexed vendor material was used where a page could not be read directly. FanBasis has a public embedded checkout demo, with merchant credential issuance still unresolved. Paygent Connect remains unidentified from first-party sources under the exact name and NMI hint. NMI links for that row document the named dependency only.
